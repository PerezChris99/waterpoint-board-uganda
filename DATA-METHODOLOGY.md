# Data Methodology

## What this platform shows

WaterPoint Board Uganda displays **community-reported** operational information about water points in one small demonstration community. Every status shown is a report, not a guarantee:

> "Reported available on 28 August 2026."

We deliberately avoid absolute claims such as "This water is safe."

## Statuses

- Available
- Partially available
- Reported unavailable
- Under maintenance
- Needs verification

## Data freshness

Each water point tracks a `last_verified_at` timestamp. The frontend will surface a data-freshness indicator (Phase 2) so users can judge how recent the reported status is before relying on it.

## What this platform does not do

- It does not certify water quality or drinking-water safety.
- It does not detect contamination.
- It does not predict infrastructure failure.
- It does not replace local water authorities or government systems.
- It is not a national-scale platform — seed data represents ~15–30 water points in one fictional community.

## Seed data

All current and future seed data is clearly fictional and created for demonstration purposes only. It does not represent verified official information about any real water point in Uganda.
